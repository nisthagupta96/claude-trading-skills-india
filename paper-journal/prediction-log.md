# Prediction log (SIMULATION ONLY)

Every morning idea is written here with what we expected (entry, stop, target, expected loss/gain after
charges, chance of hitting the target, and the reason). In the evening the "Actual" columns are filled in,
and the cause of any difference is given one code:
(a) normal market noise, (b) gap at the open, (c) data issue, (d) cost issue, (e) rule or skill error,
(f) news or event surprise. Only c, d and e count as errors.
