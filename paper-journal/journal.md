# Paper-trading journal (SIMULATION ONLY)

This is a practice notebook. No real money is used and no real orders are ever placed.
Each morning a task writes down up to 3 stock ideas ("PLANNED"). Each evening a task checks
what really happened to the prices and works out the pretend profit or loss.

Fixed rules: ₹10,000 per stock (whole shares only). Stop-loss (the price where we accept we were
wrong and sell) = entry price − 2 × ATR (ATR = the stock's average daily price swing over 14 days).
Target (the price where we take profit) = entry + 2 × the distance to the stop. At most 3 stocks at once.
At most 20 trading days per trade. Do not buy if the stock opens more than 3% above the planned price.
